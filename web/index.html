In quiet hours the autopilot doesn't look at charts or ask the brains (this PC's clock); weekends off runs from Saturday's start time to Monday's end time. Trades already open keep being managed. The equity floor blocks any trade that could take equity below it if every stop were hit; if equity reaches it, Jarvis closes his own trades and stops the autopilot. Real money is switched on by voice ("switch on live trading") and a click on the card that comes up.
