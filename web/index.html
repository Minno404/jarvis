Welcome. Let's set Jarvis up

Jarvis runs on your own Claude account and keeps everything on this PC.

Jarvis settings

Listening

Not set

Hold it anywhere, even in a game, and talk; let go to send. Works with keys and the mouse side buttons.

Triple-click the core to split. Kokoro voices run on this PC and download once (about 300 MB) the first time you pick one.

Panels

Pin up to 3 panels with the + beside a panel's x: they float over other windows while Jarvis is in the background.

Background
Darken

Or ask: "make the background a picture of a city at night".

Core model

Any .glb or .blend model works (a Cortana-style figure, a helmet...), or a .zip of a .blend with its textures. A .blend is converted with Blender, so Blender needs to be installed. Models are kept in custom/models, so updates never remove them.

Pair your phone

Scan with your phone's camera. Your phone then stays paired, and you can add it to your home screen.
